HOW IT WORKS

01

Buy In

Choose your stake from $1 to $25. Your buy-in converts to coins on the map. Higher stakes mean more coins to hunt with.

02

Hunt & Prowl

Walk around your area in real life. Your GPS position shows on the map. Find other players and AI prowlers near you.

03

Attack & Defend

Get close to enemies and attack to steal their coins. Buy shields to protect yourself from incoming attacks.

04

Cash Out

When you're ready, cash out your coins for real money. The more you prowl and steal, the bigger your payout.

GAMEPLAY BREAKDOWN

⚡

BUYING IN

  • Tap "Start Hunting" on the map screen
  • Choose from 25 tiers ($1 to $25)
  • Each dollar equals 10 coins
  • Your tier color is visible to other players
  • Once you buy in, the hunt begins
🎯

ATTACKING

  • Get within 0.25 miles of an enemy player
  • Attack panel appears automatically
  • Cycle between multiple targets in range
  • Steal all their coins on a successful hit
  • Shielded players block your attack
  • You can only attack players at your buy-in tier or lower
🛡

SHIELDS

  • Shields cost $1 each and last 10 minutes
  • Maximum 3 shields per 24 hours
  • Blocks all incoming attacks while active
  • Required when attacking certain players
  • Use them strategically at key moments
💰

COIN DROPS

  • Bonus coins appear across the map
  • Walk near a drop and tap to collect
  • Drops refresh periodically
  • Earn extra coins without attacking
  • First come, first served
💀

GETTING ELIMINATED

  • Attacked without a shield? You're eliminated
  • You lose all coins from that session
  • The attacker takes everything
  • Buy in again at any tier to keep playing
  • New players get brief spawn protection
💳

CASHING OUT

  • Your wallet tracks all transactions
  • Buy-ins, shield purchases, winnings, drops
  • Cash out your earnings for real money
  • The more you prowl, the bigger the payout
  • View stats on your profile

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is CoinProwl free to download?

▼

Yes. The app is free to download. You only spend money when you choose to buy in to a game session.

How old do I have to be to play?

▼

You must be at least 18 years old to play CoinProwl.

Where is CoinProwl available?

▼

CoinProwl is currently available in the United States only. Due to local regulations regarding skill-based gaming, the app is not available in the following states: Arizona, Arkansas, Connecticut, Delaware, Louisiana, Montana, South Carolina, South Dakota, and Tennessee. We're working to expand availability in the future.

How do I make money?

▼

Attack other players to steal their coins, collect coin drops scattered across the map, and cash out your balance for real money.

Can I lose money?

▼

Yes. If another player attacks you and you don't have a shield, you lose all your coins from that session. Only buy in with what you're comfortable losing.

How do shields work?

▼

Shields cost $1 each and protect you from attacks for 10 minutes. You can buy up to 3 shields per 24-hour period. When active, any incoming attack is blocked.

What happens if I get attacked?

▼

If you have an active shield, the attack is blocked. If you don't, you're eliminated and the attacker takes all your coins. You can buy in again to keep playing.

How close do I need to be to attack someone?

▼

You need to be within 0.25 miles (about 400 meters) of another player to attack them.

Can I attack anyone on the map?

▼

You can only attack players at your buy-in tier or lower. For example, a $25 Diamond player can attack anyone, but a $5 Bronze player can only attack $5 or below. Choose your tier wisely.

Can I play while driving?

▼

You can play as a passenger in a vehicle, but never while driving. The driver must always keep their eyes on the road. Always be aware of your surroundings when playing.

What are the different tiers?

▼

There are 25 tiers from $1 (Copper) to $25 (Diamond). Higher tiers mean more coins in play. Each tier has a unique name and color visible to other players on the map.

Is my location shared with other players?

▼

Other players can see your approximate position on the map while you have an active session. Your exact address is never shared. When you don't have an active session, you're not visible on the map.

Can I play indoors?

▼

Yes, as long as your phone has GPS signal. The game works anywhere your phone can get a location fix.

What if the app crashes or my phone dies during a session?

▼

Your session stays active on the server. When you reopen the app, you'll reconnect to your existing session. However, you're still visible on the map and can be attacked while disconnected.

How do I cash out?

▼

The cash-out feature processes your earnings directly to your wallet. Your balance is tracked in real time within the app.

How do I report a problem or another player?

▼

Contact us through the app or reach out to our support team. We take all reports seriously to keep the game fair and safe.

Contact us: support@coinprowl.com
