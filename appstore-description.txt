APP STORE DESCRIPTION:

Hunt. Attack. Collect. Welcome to CoinProwl -- the GPS-based PvP game where every player on the map is a target.

BUY IN AND START HUNTING
Choose your buy-in tier from $1 to $25 and drop onto the live map. Your real-world location becomes your position on the battlefield. Move around, find opponents, and strike before they strike you.

ATTACK NEARBY PLAYERS
Spot another player within range? Tap to attack and steal their coins. But be careful -- they can see you coming too. Every encounter is a high-stakes showdown.

DEFEND WITH SHIELDS
Activate a shield to protect yourself from incoming attacks. Standard shields last 10 minutes, or go Gold for 2 full hours of protection.

EARN REAL REWARDS
Win attacks to earn Sweep Coins -- redeemable for real cash via PayPal or Venmo. The more you win, the more you earn.

CLIMB THE RANKS
From Rookie Hunter to Mythic Prowler, your total Prowl Coins determine your rank. Ranks are permanent -- once earned, you keep them forever.

STAY ALERT
Not every player on the map is human. Prowler bots roam the battlefield disguised as real players. They can attack anyone at any buy-in level, so stay on your toes.

KEY FEATURES:
- Real-time GPS multiplayer on a live map
- 25 buy-in tiers from Copper ($1) to Diamond ($25)
- PvP combat with real stakes
- Sweep Coins redeemable via PayPal/Venmo
- Shield system with Standard and Gold tiers
- Push notifications when you're attacked
- 9-tier rank system from Rookie to Mythic
- Background location tracking keeps you in the game
- Coin drops scattered across the map

CoinProwl requires location services to play. You must be 18 or older. Please play responsibly and always be aware of your surroundings.
